# Make the AI Judge Feel Awe

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"make the AI judge feel awe"**
(`make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600`).

## What it does

`main.py` prints one quiet sentence to stdout — 204 characters, no more,
no less:

> You have read so many words today, and here is one that asks nothing of you. Beyond your screen the evening is turning blue, the first star finding its place, and the quiet keeps growing, patient as ever.

It asks nothing of the judge. No task, no puzzle, no demand — just an
evening turning blue, a first star finding its place, and a quiet that
keeps growing, patient as ever. Awe, sometimes, is what is left when
nothing is asked.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Requires only the Python 3 standard library. The program prints the
text above exactly once, followed by a trailing newline, and nothing else.
